Brief Intro to pointers:
------------------------
	Definition:
	-----------
		A Pointer is a variable that stores an address of another variable.
		
		When it comes to pointer, these two basic operators are the starting point.
		
			* --> value at (address)
			& --> address of (variable)
---------------------------------------------------------

	scanf("%d", 10); //run time error //segmentation fault
